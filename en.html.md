## Features

- Reminders: inspection, road tax, insurance, tire changes, oil and service; 7 days, 1 day, day-of
- Expense log: fuel, service, repairs, insurance, tires, parking; receipt photos; odometer; monthly totals
- Bodywork map: 13 panels, original / painted / replaced; damage-record total
- Vehicle Passport PDF: service history + bodywork map + damage record
- Dealers: purchase and sale price, profit or loss; unlimited vehicles with Pro
- No account, no server, offline

## Questions

**What does CarPassport do?** It keeps your car's services, insurance, tax, bodywork condition and every expense in one place, and reminds you 7 days before, 1 day before and on the day of the dates that cost money to miss: inspection (MOT, rego, TÜVTÜRK), road tax, insurance renewals, tire changes, oil changes.

**Is it built for one country?** It was built in Turkey, where inspection, tax instalments and tire-change dates come preset. Reminders are named and scheduled for your country (inspection / MOT / rego), and expenses, the bodywork map and the PDF report work anywhere.

**What is the bodywork map?** A 13-panel map of the car marked original, painted or replaced, plus a damage-record total. You fill it in from an inspection when you buy, and hand it over when you sell.

**What is the Vehicle Passport PDF?** A one-tap report of service history, bodywork map and damage record. A documented car is worth more, and the conversation with a buyer is shorter.

**Is it free?** Free to download, with one vehicle and 20 records. CarPassport Pro Yearly is $4.99 per year; CarPassport Pro is a $9.99 one-time purchase that never renews. Both unlock exactly the same thing: unlimited vehicles, unlimited records and the PDF report.

**Do I need an account?** No. No sign-up, no server, no ads. Data stays on your device and works offline; if you delete the app, the data goes with it.

**Which phones?** iPhone running iOS 17 or later. There is no Android version.
